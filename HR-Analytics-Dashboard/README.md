# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes workforce data to identify employee attrition and HR trends.

## Business Objective

To support workforce planning and employee retention.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* XLOOKUP
* Conditional Formatting

## Dashboard Features

* Attrition Analysis
* Department Analysis
* Workforce Distribution
* HR KPI Monitoring

## Key Insights

- The organization has 311 employees with an attrition rate of 33%.
- Employee satisfaction averaged 4/5, while performance averaged 3/5.
- Most employee exits were voluntary, driven by career growth and personal reasons.
- The Production department had the largest workforce.
- The workforce maintained a nearly balanced gender distribution.
- Most employees met performance expectations, with few exceeding them.
- Online job portals and employee referrals were the primary recruitment sources.
